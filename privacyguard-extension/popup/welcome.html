PrivacyGuard

Real-time privacy guardian for AI conversations

What This Does

PrivacyGuard scans your messages before you send them to AI agents like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. It detects sensitive personal information and warns you before it's too late.

Supported Sites

ChatGPT Claude Gemini Copilot Perplexity Pi

How to Use

1
Start chatting normally. PrivacyGuard works automatically in the background on supported AI sites.
2
Get warned in real-time. Before any message is sent, it's scanned for sensitive data. A warning appears if risks are found.
3
Choose your action: Block & Learn (+5 pts), Edit & Resend (+2 pts), or Send Anyway (-1 pt).
4
Click the shield icon in your toolbar to see your privacy score, scan history, permissions, and custom rules.

100% Private & Local

All scanning happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server. Your messages never leave your device.
